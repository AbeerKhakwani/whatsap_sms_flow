Frequently Asked Questions

What brands do you accept?

We accept Pakistani and international designer brands — Sana Safinaz, Khaadi, Agha Noor, Maria B, Elan, HSY, Zara, and many more. Not sure? Just ask us on WhatsApp!

How much does it cost to sell?

Listing is completely free. We only take an 18% commission when your item sells. You keep 82% of the sale price.

Can I send a voice note instead of typing?

Absolutely! Just record a voice note describing your item and our AI will transcribe and extract all the details. It's the fastest way to list.

How many photos do I need?

At least 3 — front view, back view, and the designer label. More photos = faster approval and quicker sales!

How do I ship my item when it sells?

We provide a prepaid shipping label right on WhatsApp. Just pack your item and drop it off — no shipping costs for you!

When do I get paid?

Payments are processed after the buyer receives the item. We pay weekly via your preferred method.
