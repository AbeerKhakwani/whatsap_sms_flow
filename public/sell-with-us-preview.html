Common Questions

Why can't I use the old platform?

We're upgrading to a much better selling experience! Our new platform will let you list items via WhatsApp (voice notes + AI) or a dedicated Seller Portal. During the transition, email submissions keep things moving.

What are the 10 monthly spots?

To ensure quality during our transition, we're accepting up to 10 new listings per month. Once all spots are filled, submissions re-open the following month. Submit early to secure your spot!

What brands do you accept?

Pakistani designers (Sana Safinaz, Khaadi, Agha Noor, Maria B, Elan, HSY, Asim Jofa, Baroque, and more) plus international brands (Zara, Mango, H&M, etc). Not sure? Just ask us!

How much does it cost?

Listing is completely free. We only take an 18% commission when your item sells. You keep 82% of the sale price. Shipping is on us — we provide a prepaid label.

How long does approval take?

You'll receive a response regarding approval within 4–5 business days of emailing us.

How do I ship when it sells?

We send a prepaid shipping label to your email. Just pack your item, stick on the label, and drop it off at any shipping location. No cost to you!

When do I get paid?

Payments are processed after the buyer receives the item. We pay weekly via your preferred method (Zelle, Venmo, or bank transfer).
